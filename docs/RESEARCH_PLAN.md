# Research plan

Generated from the adversarially verified research-gap study (Aug 2026).
Constraint: fully simulation-based, one RTX 5090, solo, 6 to 12 months.

## Problem statement

LIBERO-Plus and LangGap established that single-arm VLAs largely ignore their instructions, but that audit only ever perturbs language that names an object, and it cannot express the semantics that make language necessary for a team: role binding across identical-looking targets, precedence, exclusion, and mid-episode reassignment. Every multi-robot VLA/VLM coordination result now appearing (CHORUS, CoVLA, MA-VLA, all MARS entrants) reports only aggregate team success, and every one of them coordinates implicitly through a shared workspace view - the exact condition under which the instruction channel can be silently inert while the team solves the task from geometry alone. No causal instruction-grounding audit exists for a robot team, and no metric exists that survives MARL's intrinsic multi-agent stochasticity, so those numbers are currently unfalsifiable. This project builds the task family, the construct-validity device, and the metric that make language grounding in multi-robot policies a measured quantity rather than an assumption.

## Contributions

1. A cooperative Isaac Lab task family (role binding over identical targets, precedence, exclusion, mid-episode reassignment) where each task ships a machine-checked LANGUAGE-NECESSITY CERTIFICATE: a full-state, instruction-blind MAPPO oracle trained to convergence whose assignment accuracy must sit at chance with a tight CI. This converts 'we designed the task so language is needed' from an assertion into a measured property, and I found no precedent for it in either the single-agent audit line or the multi-robot line. These four semantic categories are structurally inexpressible in a single-arm audit.

2. The Coordination Sensitivity Index (CSI): behavioral/outcome divergence under a counterfactual instruction intervention, normalized by divergence under a physics-seed change alone. Raw success-rate deltas (the Contrast Sets / LIBERO-Plus primitive) are uninterpretable in MARL because multi-agent stochasticity alone produces large trajectory divergence; the seed normalization is the specific thing that makes counterfactual auditing portable to teams. Reported per semantic category, not just in aggregate.

3. The pre-registered headline: a DISSOCIATION that does not follow from the single-agent literature - per-agent atomic prompting is language-sensitive on role binding but near-inert on precedence, because emergent collision avoidance satisfies precedence constraints for free. A team can therefore be provably language-blind on assignment while appearing language-sensitive on aggregate success. This is the finding that stops a reviewer calling the work LIBERO-Plus transferred to MARL.

4. A controlled four-way comparison of conditioning interfaces (global instruction broadcast / per-agent atomic prompts / frozen VLM commander emitting per-agent sub-goal tokens / frozen sentence-embedding) trained under identical MARL, plus evidence that CSI - not success rate - predicts held-out generalization to unseen instruction-role compositions and unseen team sizes. Shipped as an open harness, with a bounded zero-shot Smorphi arm on the two extreme conditions showing the sim verdict survives reality.

## Method

Everything language-side is frozen; the only trained parameters are small MARL policies, which is what makes this fit one 5090. Isaac Lab DirectMARLEnv (PettingZoo Parallel API, skrl MAPPO/HAPPO) over the researcher's existing stack: PhysX raycast LiDAR plus a shared semantic occupancy grid, continuous mecanum kinematics, N=2-4 agents, thousands of parallel envs, no RTX camera rendering in the training loop. Each agent receives ego-state, local raycast returns, the grid crop, an agent-ID/role token, and a language conditioning vector produced by one of four interfaces; instruction embeddings are computed once offline and cached, so the text encoder never runs during rollouts. A permutation-invariant teammate-entity encoder makes the policy team-size agnostic, which is what enables the held-out-team-size generalization test. The critical architectural decision, forced by the month-1 throughput benchmark: the VLM commander arm uses a render-free SYMBOLIC stand-in during training (a distilled, cached sub-goal-token vocabulary produced offline by the real 3B VLM over the finite scene/instruction space), with the actual VLM invoked only at evaluation - firing a 3-7B VLM at 1-2 Hz across rendered parallel envs collapses Isaac Lab throughput by one to two orders of magnitude and would eat the year. The repair arm (InfoNCE between instruction embedding and realized trajectory with teammates' instructions as negatives, plus MA-VLA-style role-permutation training) is run and reported explicitly as a baseline-repair CONTROL, not as a contribution, because it is standard contrastive grounding plus Arm Shuffle with the domain swapped.

## Evaluation plan

Baselines: language-free MARL (floor), full-state oracle assignment (ceiling), CHORUS-style shared-backbone BC on scripted multi-robot demonstrations (the VLA contact point), and a prompted-VLM-planner-plus-scripted-controller. Perturbations, always with scene, spawn and physics seed frozen: instruction swap between agents, role permutation, semantically-null paraphrase, contradiction, and mid-episode reassignment. Primary table is success rate versus CSI across four architectures x five perturbations x four task families; secondary is a correlation plot of CSI against held-out generalization (unseen instruction-role compositions, unseen team size N=4 trained at N=2-3) with paired-seed CIs. Coordination metrics reported alongside success throughout: deadlock rate, idle time, redundant-action rate, cross-agent interference, role stability, assignment accuracy. Statistics are the differentiator and are pre-registered: >=200 seeded episodes per condition, counterfactual pairs sharing an identical physical seed so every comparison is paired, the EPISODE as the clustering unit with hierarchical/cluster-robust bootstrap CIs (agents within an episode are not independent trials - the single most common error in the multi-robot papers being audited), paired bootstrap for architecture deltas, and Holm correction across the perturbation family. Construct validity is enforced before any language result is reported via the certificate: if the instruction-blind oracle beats chance, a symmetry-breaking convention exists and the task is redesigned. Hardware arm: 2-3 Smorphi, two extreme conditions only, ~60-100 seeded episodes, episode-clustered bootstrap, zero-shot from the existing Isaac Sim 5.1 pipeline.

## Milestones

- Months 1-1.5 - Certificate and throughput gate. Port the existing occupancy-grid fleet into an Isaac Lab DirectMARLEnv at N=2 with two variants (identical-target role binding, precedence). Train the full-state instruction-blind MAPPO oracle over 5 seeds and prove assignment accuracy sits at chance with a tight CI; randomize spawn order, target IDs and agent indices until it does. In parallel benchmark env FPS with a 3B VLM in the loop versus the symbolic stand-in to lock the commander architecture. GO/NO-GO: certificate holds at chance AND a sentence-embedding-conditioned policy reaches high success with near-zero CSI on the precedence variant. If CSI is already high on the first architecture, pivot the paper to the conditioning-architecture comparison as the primary contribution.

- Months 2-3 - CSI v1 and the dissociation probe. Train all four conditioning architectures on both variants, 5 seeds each. Compute CSI with paired-seed CIs and produce the first success-versus-CSI table. Explicitly test the pre-registered dissociation (per-agent prompting sensitive on role binding, inert on precedence) and log an alternative headline if it does not appear. This is the point at which the paper's identity is fixed.

- Months 4-5 - Full task family and baselines. Add exclusion and mid-episode reassignment tasks, extend to N=2-4, run the complete five-perturbation suite. Bring up the CHORUS-style shared-backbone BC baseline (scripted demonstrators) and the prompted-VLM-planner-plus-scripted-controller. Freeze the perturbation protocol here; no new perturbation types after this point.

- Months 6-7 - Does CSI predict generalization. Held-out instruction-role compositions and held-out team size via the permutation-invariant teammate encoder; correlate CSI against generalization gap with paired bootstrap. Run the repair-control arm (InfoNCE grounding regularizer plus role permutation) and report whether raising CSI costs in-distribution success. Second headline plot lands here.

- Months 8-9 - Reality check, bounded. Zero-shot revalidation of final checkpoints in the existing Isaac Sim 5.1 raycast-LiDAR harness, then the Smorphi arm: 2-3 robots, the two extreme conditions only (highest-CSI and near-zero-CSI architecture), ~60-100 seeded episodes with episode-clustered bootstrap. Hard stop at 4 weeks; this arm is droppable without damaging the core claim.

- Months 10-12 - Harness release, reviewer-proofing, submission. Open-source the task family, certificates, perturbation suite and CSI implementation. Write the related-work section defensively around Contrast Sets (CoRL 2025), LIBERO-Plus and LangGap as explicit methodological ancestry rather than competitors. Reserve 3 weeks for the ablation cleanup reviewers will demand (CSI normalization choice, episode-clustering versus naive per-agent statistics, sensitivity to number of seeds). Target CoRL 2027 deadline with NeurIPS D&B as the immediate resubmission path.

## Risks and mitigations

1) The headline is largely pre-announced - LIBERO-Plus and LangGap already published 'policies ignore language', so 'teams ignore language too' is not a paper. Mitigation: the contribution is pre-registered as the certificate + CSI + four-way architecture comparison + the precedence/role-binding dissociation, and month 2-3 is structured to find that dissociation or to declare an alternative differentiated finding before any further investment. 2) Counterfactual instruction auditing is itself not new (Contrast Sets, CoRL 2025). Mitigation: cite it as ancestry in the abstract, claim only the multi-agent-specific semantics and the seed-normalized metric; do not use the phrase 'first causal audit'. 3) VLM-in-the-loop throughput collapse is the single biggest schedule risk. Mitigation: it is measured in week 4 and the render-free distilled symbolic commander is the default, with the real VLM confined to evaluation. 4) Task design failure - if the instruction-blind oracle beats chance, the certificate fails and the task is invalid. Mitigation: two weeks of redesign are budgeted; a failed first design is itself reportable evidence that hand-designed 'language-necessary' tasks usually are not. 5) Null result on the fix arm (grounding regularizer raises CSI but does not improve generalization). Mitigation: the fix arm is already framed as a control, so a null there is a finding about CSI's predictive validity, not a failure of the paper. 6) Impact ceiling - this lands at CoRL/D&B/RA-L, not RSS or a NeurIPS main-track headline, and multi-robot language conditioning is a small subcommunity. Accepted deliberately: the objective is ONE strong publishable paper in 12 months on one GPU, and this is the only survivor whose probability of delivering that exceeds ~70%. 7) Scoop risk is real but the lowest in the set - MA-VLA and CHORUS authors have no incentive to audit their own language channel - and the CSI harness plus the trained language-conditioned fleet are directly reusable as the diagnostic infrastructure for a TeamGRPO-style follow-up in year 2.

## Nearest prior work (from the novelty verification)

- LIBERO-Plus: In-depth Robustness Analysis of Vision-Language-Action Models (arXiv 2510.13626; CVPR 2026) - 7-dimension perturbation audit incl. language; concludes VLAs largely ignore instructions. Single-arm, language only names objects.
- LangGap: Diagnosing and Closing the Language Gap in Vision-Language-Action Models (arXiv 2603.00592, Feb 2026 preprint) - systematic semantic-perturbation benchmark showing SOTA VLAs 'largely ignore language instructions', plus a data-augmentation fix (0%->90% single-task). Single-agent tabletop. NOT cited by the proposer; strongest missed prior.
- Contrast Sets for Evaluating Language-Guided Robot Policies (Anwar, Gupta, Thomason; PMLR v270 / CoRL 2025) - the methodological ancestor: minimal counterfactual perturbations to instruction and observation, scored by relative performance change, on simulated manipulation + physical VLN. Single robot, no coordination.
- Robust Skills, Brittle Grounding: Diagnosing Restricted Generalization in VLA Policies via Multi-Object Picking (arXiv 2602.24143, Feb 2026) - held-out object-location pairings break visual shortcuts without changing spatial difficulty; decomposes primitive execution from instruction-conditioned success. Single-arm.
- MA-VLA: Multi-Arm Vision-Language-Action Model for Collaboration and Compositional Generalization (arXiv 2608.25864, Aug 2026) - per-arm atomic prompts + Arm Shuffle. VERIFIED via full-text fetch: no test-time instruction perturbation, no language-blind/no-instruction control, no language-sensitivity metric, and related work cites no audit literature.
- Also checked and cleared: ICCO (IROS 2025, 2503.12122), Prompting Robot Teams with Natural Language (RA-L, 2509.24575), CoLF (2602.07776), MA-VLCM (2603.15418), MAVIC/Robust Instruction Compliance in Cooperative MARL (2605.12655), ALEM open-ended multi-agent coordination benchmark (2606.08340) - all method/benchmark papers with language conditioning but no causal instruction-grounding measurement.

Remaining delta: A real delta remains, but it is narrower than the proposal claims, and the proposal must be re-pitched honestly.

WHAT IS NOT NEW (the proposal overclaims here): "Counterfactual instruction perturbation as a causal audit" is a solved, published measurement paradigm. Contrast Sets (CoRL 2025) already formalized minimal instruction-vs-observation perturbations scored by relative performance change; LIBERO-Plus and LangGap already delivered the "policies ignore language" verdict; Robust Skills/Brittle Grounding already builds tasks where visual shortcuts are provably insufficient and separates skill execution from instruction conditioning. The claim "this is the first causal instruction-grounding audit" is false in general - it is only first FOR A TEAM. Also, the fix arm is the weakest part: InfoNCE between instruction embedding and realized trajectory is standard contrastive grounding, and role-permutation training is literally MA-VLA's Arm Shuffle with the domain swapped from arms to a mobile fleet. That arm should be framed as a baseline-repair control, not a contribution.

WHAT IS STILL NEW: (1) Multi-agent-specific linguistic content. Every existing audit perturbs language that names an object or attribute. Role binding across identical-looking targets, precedence ("B waits until A clears the cell"), exclusion, and mid-episode reassignment are semantic categories that are structurally inexpressible in a single-arm audit, and they fail differently - a team can satisfy precedence by emergent collision avoidance with zero instruction reading, a failure mode single-agent contrast sets cannot even represent. (2) The language-necessity certificate. A full-state, instruction-blind oracle whose trained ceiling must sit at chance-level assignment is a construct-validity device I found no precedent for in either the single-agent audit line or the multi-robot line; it converts "we designed the task so language is needed" from an assertion into a measured property. (3) The Coordination Sensitivity Index normalizing counterfactual divergence by same-seed-change divergence. Existing metrics are raw success-rate deltas, which are uninterpretable in MARL where multi-agent stochasticity alone produces large trajectory divergence; the normalization is the specific thing that makes the audit portable to teams. (4) A controlled four-way conditioning-architecture comparison (global broadcast / per-agent atomic prompts / frozen VLM commander / sentence-embedding) under identical MARL, which nobody has run - MA-VLA compares against non-language baselines, not against alternative conditioning interfaces.

REVIEWER RISK TO PLAN FOR: the headline result is largely pre-announced by LIBERO-Plus and LangGap, so "we found teams ignore language too" is not a paper. The contribution must be carried by the certificate + CSI + architecture comparison, and ideally by a multi-agent-specific finding that does NOT follow from the single-agent literature - e.g., that per-agent atomic prompting scores high CSI on role binding but ~0 on precedence, or that a policy can be language-blind on assignment while appearing language-sensitive on aggregate success. Without such a differentiated finding, a reviewer can reasonably call this LIBERO-Plus/Contrast-Sets transferred to MARL.

## Hardware-arm note

The original proposal included a physical Smorphi arm. The project is now fully simulation-based (no hardware available); the replacement is a cross-fidelity robustness arm, zero-shot transfer of final checkpoints to a high-fidelity Isaac evaluation with RTX rendering, the real 3B VLM in the loop at evaluation, PhysX raycast sensing, and localisation-noise sweeps.
